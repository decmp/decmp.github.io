The Nintendo 64 has a MIPS CPU and one of the oldest and best-documented decomp communities. Most games were built
with SGI's IDO compiler or with GCC.

## What this page will cover

- The CPU and the compilers: IDO versions and flags, and GCC-built games.
- Splitting a ROM with [splat](https://github.com/ethteck/splat), and how a typical project is laid out.
- Drafting with [m2c](https://github.com/matt-kempster/m2c) (`mips` targets) and diffing with
  [asm-differ](https://github.com/simonlindholm/asm-differ) or [objdiff](https://github.com/encounter/objdiff).
- libultra, the SDK, and why projects share its matched code.
- Overlays and compressed segments.
