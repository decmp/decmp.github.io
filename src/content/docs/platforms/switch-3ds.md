Newer consoles bring much bigger binaries, modern C++ and newer compilers. The ideas of a matching decomp are the
same, but the scale changes how projects are organized. [objdiff](https://github.com/encounter/objdiff) supports
ARM64 for Switch and ARM for 3DS.

## What this page will cover

- Switch: ARM64 code, and identifying the compiler and its version.
- 3DS: ARM code and the compilers games were built with.
- C++ at scale: templates, inlining and the standard library.
- Choosing a slice of the game to match first.
